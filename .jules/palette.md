## 2024-05-21 - Custom Input and Control Group Accessibility
**Learning:** The `Toggle` and `ColorInput` components visually hid native inputs but lacked adequate keyboard focus states or clear `aria-label` definitions, making them inaccessible to keyboard/screen reader users. Furthermore, custom `SegmentedControl` components were effectively a group of buttons without the necessary `radiogroup` semantics to convey selection context to screen readers.
**Action:** When styling custom representations of hidden native inputs, always use `peer` (on the `<input>`) and `peer-focus-visible` (on the custom UI element) from Tailwind to sync keyboard focus states, while ensuring explicit `aria-label` definitions exist. Always wrap custom single-choice segmented buttons in a `role="radiogroup"` wrapper, providing `role="radio"` and `aria-checked` properties to its child buttons.
